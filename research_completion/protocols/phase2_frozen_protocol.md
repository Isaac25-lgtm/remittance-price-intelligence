## Questions, information and selection

E1, E2, E3 and E4 retain their original definitions. The primary analysis is retrospective, conditional on unique valid reobservation, using end-of-survey covariates. Current public release dates must never be inferred from nominal quarter labels. A release-aware early-warning analysis proceeds only for waves with independently proven predictor availability before target collection. Otherwise its status is NOT_ESTIMABLE.

All existing years are development evidence. Annual expanding-window refits retain the legacy embargo. The last four available training target quarters form chronological validation; only earlier observations train tuning/calibration candidates. Finite candidates and the preference rule are frozen in configs/r01_phase2_publication.yaml. Calibration methods are selected on development validation, hence validation estimates are selection-biased; annual test scores remain retrospective. No model is operationally selected unless timing passes.

Transfers hold out deterministic 20% corridor/provider groups, rebuild outcome histories without those groups and retain only training-global fallback for unseen histories. Transfer evaluates historical geographic/provider generalisation, not independent chronological confirmation. No held-out outcomes may enter any history, even later within the test year.

## Uncertainty, robustness and public boundary

Paired Brier differences and absolute metrics use 1,000 corridor/provider draws, circular moving four-quarter blocks and crossed product-of-independent-cluster weights (pigeonhole sensitivity). The latter is not claimed an exact universal multiway interval. Report few-quarter limitations. Capacity is ranked within each origin quarter with deterministic source-row tie order. All alternatives are exploratory and no multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory claims are made.

Descriptive sensitivities include ±0.05/0.1 threshold bands, 5%, USD500, strict/reduced unique identity, two-quarter horizon, transparency, negative FX and collection-quarter labels. Fitted sensitivities compare the same four predeclared strong families. Price-component and continuous-cost analysis are secondary: last cost, ridge and robust tree regression if valid data support them. No service-exit or causal modelling is justified.

Missingness: evaluate separate reobservation models; MAR IPW is an assumption scenario with train-only propensity fitting, clipping and effective sample size. Pattern-mixture scenarios vary unknown failure log odds ±1/2 relative to observed rates. E2 bounds remain assumption-free for the recorded eligible cohort. Reduced identity is a collision-audited sensitivity, never a confirmed correction. Manual public rebrand adjudication cannot promote links without source evidence.

Safe outputs contain aggregates only, no provider names, source rows, service identifiers, individual predictions or binaries. Private artifacts live in ignored private_runtime or data/processed. Source literature PDFs are private reading copies and excluded from the handoff. Every unperformed requirement receives an explicit ledger status, not an empty synthetic artifact.

Future-wave evaluation is frozen separately and rejects old labels. Independent confirmation requires a genuinely new exact adjacent wave pair and valid timing, with no tuning on its outcomes. No such period is assumed to exist.
