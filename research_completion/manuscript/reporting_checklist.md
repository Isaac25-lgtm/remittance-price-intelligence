# Reporting checklist

Finance/development observational measurement reporting, not claimed clinical TRIPOD compliance. Estimands, population, source dates, quote/transaction distinction, identity, attrition, timing, leakage, preprocessing folds, model-selection history, calibration, group/temporal limits, paired inference, negative results, missing-data assumptions, availability, licensing and authorship declarations are documented. Remaining gaps are explicitly marked in publication_gates.csv and REMAINING_BLOCKERS.md. Source PDFs and row-level artifacts are not redistributed.
