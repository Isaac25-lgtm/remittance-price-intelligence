# Decision value boundaries

At the 20% all-origin review capacity, the selected policy reviews 4,905 of 24,556 eligible records;4,889 outcomes are unknown overall. All-failure recall bounds are 0.216707-0.572562. No population-wide policy ranking, avoided harm or consumer saving is established. [P03]

The original conditional review benchmark ranks a sample defined using future reobservation; it is a retrospective observed-sample diagnostic. The corrected all-origin scenario keeps known failures/stays and unknown reviewed/not-reviewed records separate. Recall bounds allocate unknown failure states adversarially; conditional probabilities do not automatically transport into the unknown subset. Review costs, service availability and consumer transactions are unobserved. Sensitivity costs are scenarios, not money saved.

For a monitoring analyst, the useful result is that an 81% matched-record statistic does not establish an 80% all-origin guarantee, even when at least one affordable corridor quote often remains surveyed. Rechecking identity/coverage and reporting missing successors is justified as a measurement practice; efficacy of any priority queue is not established. No deployed or operational model is selected.
