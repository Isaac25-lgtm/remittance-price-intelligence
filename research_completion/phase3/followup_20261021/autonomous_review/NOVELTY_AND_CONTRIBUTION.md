# Contribution assessment after autonomous evidence investigation

## Exact candidate contribution

The candidate is a retrospective measurement application: in one fixed RPW extract, requiring unchanged recorded speed/network greatly reduces exact-next-quarter quote linkage and widens all-origin cheap-to-cheap bounds, while conditional continuity changes little. The new extension locates the difference across source eras and instrument/payout composition, and establishes the limits of accessible historical documentation for a stratified 60-case sample. It does not introduce bounding theory, identify true product replacement or produce an operational model.

Question 1: **Does a consequential empirical phenomenon survive beyond arithmetic?** The concentration is real in the recorded panel: extra width is 2.22 pp before the first classification target, 22.17 pp between the two changes and 18.16 pp after the second. It is also larger for bank/account payout than cash. This is more informative than reporting only pooled interval widening. However, composition changes and documented recoding can generate that pattern. The exercise has not isolated an economic phenomenon, a new recording mechanism, a causal effect or an actionable monitoring improvement. Official RPW reports already announced service splitting. Magnitude and temporal alignment alone do not establish sufficient scientific originality.

Question 2: **Is it interpretable without verified stable products?** Yes, as sensitivity of record-defined continuity to explicit key choices on a finite survey extract. No, as actual survival, withdrawal, quality change or durable affordability of economically identical services. All 60 cases remain unresolved. A narrow record-based estimand is coherent and the repository is useful; that fact alone does not make the application substantial enough for an original article.
