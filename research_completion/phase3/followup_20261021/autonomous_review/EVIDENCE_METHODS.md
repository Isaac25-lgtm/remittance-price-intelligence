## Two machine passes

Pass 1 is AI-assisted documentary extraction and case-specific assessment, transparently performed by this coding assistant, using fixed schema/prompt version `R01_AUTONOMOUS_DOCUMENTARY_V1`. Record scope, date/effective interval, corridor, provider, covered attributes, alternatives and a counterargument. No new paid model/API calls or human adjudicators.

Pass 2 is a separate adversarial deterministic software audit of that extraction: challenge missing temporal evidence, incomplete corridor/provider/bundle coverage, ambiguous or absent successors, raw-label-only reasoning, contradictory documents and source-classification shifts. It is not an independent investigator or an inter-rater study. Preserve pass-1 proposals and audit reasons even when downgraded.

Grades: A exact dated full-bundle documentary coverage of both periods; B near-period provider/corridor evidence with partial bundle specificity; C generic, modern, one-sided or provider-only context; D no accessible pertinent provider documentary support. Global methodology alone does not upgrade D. Do not combine several B/C sources into A by confidence scoring. A partial time interval remains partial, not continuous quarterly coverage.

Final machine statuses: EVIDENCE_SUPPORTS_SAME, EVIDENCE_SUPPORTS_DIFFERENT, UNRESOLVED_INSUFFICIENT_HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE, NOT_REVIEWABLE. SAME requires grade A, explicit support for both periods, the ordered corridor/provider/funding/access/payout bundle, no unresolved competing successor and no material contradiction. DIFFERENT requires direct documentary evidence of distinct products in the relevant periods, not a missing source key, provider closure alone or changed speed/network vocabulary. Inadequate case-specific evidence remains UNRESOLVED, even if every case does. A reviewer can still inspect a missing-target case, so absence alone is not NOT_REVIEWABLE.
