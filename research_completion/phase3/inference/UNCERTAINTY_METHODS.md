The finite source panel has 37 observed waves, 36 eligible-origin waves and 35 with valid E1 pairs. For that panel, conditional counts and identification bounds are deterministic given identity and valid costs. They do not require an IID binomial sampling interval. For hypothetical temporal generalisation, let n_t be a wave numerator, d_t its denominator, theta=sum(n_t)/sum(d_t), and z_t=(n_t-theta*d_t)/mean(d_t). With T eligible-origin waves, the Bartlett HAC sensitivity uses V=(gamma0+2*sum((1-k/(L+1))*gamma_k))/T, gamma_k=sum(z_t*z_(t-k))/T, fixed L in{0, 1, 4}, and t_(T-1) critical values. Source-calendar qidx is retained; no missing wave is compressed into a successor. Eligible-origin waves are consecutive through 2025Q1 here; the final missing-target wave contributes to E2 but has zero E1 denominator. Bounds are clipped to[0, 1] only for probability displays, not paired effects.

For strict-minus-core width, numerator is additional unknown records and denominator is the identical all-origin count. Entire wave totals absorb arbitrary contemporaneous within-wave provider/corridor dependence; the method still assumes sufficiently regular weak temporal dependence and does not validate that assumption with this small, changing survey panel. Nonstationarity, provider persistence across waves, group imbalance and survey changes limit interpretation. Report all fixed-lag results, not the most favourable interval. No robust 95% coverage claim or confirmatory p-value is made.
