# Recorded remittance-quote continuity under alternative identity definitions

**Conditional research dossier, 21 October 2026. Not approved for journal submission.** Current decision: `CONDITIONAL_HOLD_EXTERNAL_VALIDATION`. This document is a complete account of the defensible retrospective result and its unresolved scientific gates. It is not an externally validated product-survival manuscript.

## Abstract

**Question.** How sensitive is exact-next-quarter affordability continuity in Remittance Prices Worldwide (RPW) to the definition of a continuing recorded offer? **Design.** Retrospective finite-panel analysis of 204,469 quotes in 37 observed waves from 2016Q2 through 2025Q3, with 2025Q2 absent. Two deterministic identities were applied to the same 45,343 eligible cheap USD200 origins. Missing successor outcomes were retained as unknown. A blinded autonomous documentary investigation covered 60 deliberately stratified cases, followed by adversarial software checks and freezing before unblinding. **Results.** Core identity produced 36,429 valid pairs, 29,546 remaining at or below 3%, conditional continuity 81.1057%, and all-origin bounds 65.1611%–84.8201%. Adding unchanged speed/network produced 30,570 valid pairs and conditional continuity 81.0468%, with bounds 54.6413%–87.2218%. The 12.9215 pp widening equals the additional unknown share. Extra width was 2.22 pp before the Q2 2021 target classification change, 22.17 pp between changes and 18.16 pp after the Q4 2022 target change. All 60 documentary cases remained unresolved; grades A/B/C/D were 0/2/47/11. **Interpretation.** Similar conditional estimates conceal substantial record-definition sensitivity. The result does not establish stable economic products, a new statistical method, a global matching-error rate or predictive operational value. Substantial originality remains unconfirmed; the original-article route is on hold.

## 1. Motivation and scientific scope

Aggregate corridor costs and consumer options are not the same quantity as the continuation of an individual survey record. A matched record can be informative, but both matching and nonobservation need explicit interpretation. This study asks a narrow measurement question on one historical extract. It does not estimate remittances actually transacted, the distribution of consumer prices paid, provider market exit, causal effects of digitalization or durable access to economically identical products.

RPW firm/service analyses already exist. Beck, Janfils and Kpodar study firm-level remittance fees; Borra and Saritha examine digital pricing and survey expansion; Lim's inspected author v1.1 studies digital discounts and availability gradients. Product-replacement selection and standard missing-outcome bounds also predate this application. The possible contribution is the empirical scale and composition of record-defined continuity sensitivity. Whether that contribution is substantial enough for a standalone article is unresolved. ## 3. Identity, outcomes and uncertainty

Core identity uses ordered corridor, normalized provider, payment instrument, access point, pickup method/location and basket. Strict additionally requires unchanged recorded speed and receiving-network coverage. Only explicit normalization rules are applied; no speculative alias or AI match replaces them. Unique, valid-price, exact-next-quarter pairs have known stay/fail outcomes. Absent keys, ambiguity, unavailable prices, gaps and other prescribed invalid states remain unknown; they are not automatically failures or exits.

For origin count N, valid pairs V, stays S, failures F and unknown U, V=S+F and N=V+U. Conditional continuity is S/V. With binary missing outcomes unrestricted, the finite-panel all-origin interval is [S/N,(S+U)/N]. It is conditional on the chosen record identity and is not a confidence interval. Making additional valid links unknown mechanically widens it. Sampling inference to all migrants, market products or future quarters requires additional assumptions unsupported here. Previously specified HAC lag and crossed-dependence analyses remain assumption-dependent sensitivity evidence, including adverse simulation results; no favourable lag or model was selected in this extension.

Source eras align with target Q2 2021 and Q4 2022 classification changes documented in official reports. Funding/payout/provider-type/region groupings and equal-group weights describe composition. A minimum 30-origin reporting floor is disclosed with complete group support. These comparisons do not identify recoding causes or treatment effects.

## 5. Results

The [primary and sensitivity tables](IDENTITY_SENSITIVITY.md) give exact denominators. Core has S=29,546, F=6,883 and U=8,914. Strict has S=24,776, F=5,794 and U=14,773. Of 5,859 newly unknown outcomes, 4,770 were core stays and 1,089 core failures. Conditional continuity changes by about −0.059 pp, while interval width increases by 12.9215 pp. This contrast is descriptive, not evidence that the stricter key is a corrected identity rule.

Before the first target-era change 371/16,726 core-valid links become unknown under strict, versus 273/1,247 at that boundary, 1,684/7,595 between changes, 209/1,481 at the second boundary and 3,322/18,294 afterwards. Bank/account payout has 16.84 pp extra width versus 8.87 pp for cash; bank funding has 16.60 pp versus 11.53 pp for cash. The data cannot allocate these differences between actual products, survey coverage and recording changes. No problematic period is dropped to stabilize the result.

Core quote-weighted conditional continuity is 81.11%, versus 69.54% with equal corridor and 63.21% with equal provider weights. These are different descriptive estimands, not population corrections. All groups and zero-valid support are disclosed. For assumed false-positive linkage fractions of 1%, 5% and 10% of core valid links, adversarial all-origin envelopes become 64.36%–85.62%, 61.15%–88.84% and 57.13%–92.85%. Those fractions are illustrative assumptions, not estimated errors. Full arithmetic and interpretation are in the sensitivity report.

Documentary support was A=0, B=2, C=47, D=11; all 60 final statuses were unresolved. Even the 12 unchanged-match cases lacked full two-quarter product support (one B, eleven C). No missing target was converted to an affordability failure and no alternative was promoted. The sample supports a feasibility statement about documentation, not a validation accuracy or representative missingness estimate. Its economic-product interpretation remains uncorroborated.

## 6. Models and numerical reproducibility

The predictive analyses remain secondary, retrospective and adverse to deployment. Thirteen inspected target quarters do not constitute a future holdout after selection. The selected policy Brier of 0.130763 versus raw spline 0.136151 does not yield robust superiority: the joint difference was −0.005388 with interval [−0.011730,+0.000457]. Simultaneous coverage was 0.86. Source release clocks do not establish historical lead time. No fresh outcomes or classifier fits were added.

The original strict 1e-9 comparator remains unchanged: 14 tables match, two differ only in timing, and 14 outputs differ (12 numerical/rank-derived tables and two environment-metadata files). Same-environment Windows equality and passing Linux fixture CI do not establish full historical cross-platform equivalence. ## 7. Interpretation and limitations

Recorded-key continuity can be measured exactly on this extract; its economic meaning depends on comparability that was not verified. Official service-splitting changes make record instability plausible but do not identify causal shares. Accessible provider/regulator documents mostly describe corporate history, generic channels or a single period. Public archives, language/extraction limits, undated pages and redirects constrain ascertainment. All AI assessments remain nonhuman documentary plausibility judgments and may contain extraction errors. Raw labels, document counts and completed tasks cannot repair these limitations.

The publication decision is `CONDITIONAL_HOLD_EXTERNAL_VALIDATION`, with a NO-GO recommendation for original-article submission now. A permanent impossibility has not been proved; neither has sufficient originality for a measurement note. Reproducibility code, transparent denominators and this conditional account remain useful. No additional routine model search, favourable-era deletion or AI-confidence threshold is justified as a remedy. [Decision](FINAL_PUBLICATION_DECISION.md).
