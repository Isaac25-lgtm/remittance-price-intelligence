## Reproduction limits in the inspected competitor package

Static inspection also shows version alignment limits: the paper's R8 describes a minimum of 100 other-corridor observations, whereas the supplied code explicitly retains positive leave-out counts without that threshold; paper/output R10 firm effects and R11 corridor-year effects have different numbering/specification details in code. The script does not explicitly cap the input at 2025Q1, so applying it to our later workbook would not reproduce its original cohort without an additional restriction. These observations are not grounds to dismiss the study or an empirical refutation of its reported coefficients. They reinforce why we do not claim exact reproduction or numerical superiority from this overlap audit.
