## What you are deciding

The question is whether the origin and candidate successor describe a historically comparable full service bundle. Consider provider identity, ordered sending/receiving countries, funding instrument, access point, receiving/pickup method and location, speed and network. A documented change can justify DIFFERENT_BUNDLE; unchanged labels alone cannot justify SAME_BUNDLE. More than one candidate may prevent identifying a unique successor. Do not collapse account/cash/wallet or payment differences merely because the same firm offers them.

- **SAME_BUNDLE:** sufficiently supported historical evidence establishes comparable offers in both periods, including relevant material attributes. Explain how the evidence covers both periods and why alternatives do not prevent the judgment.
- **DIFFERENT_BUNDLE:** historical evidence supports a material product distinction; specify it. A changed source field alone may reflect recording practice and is insufficient to establish an economic change.
- **UNRESOLVED:** plausible match or difference, but evidence is absent, partial, contradictory, undated or insufficiently specific. This is an acceptable scientific result. State exactly what is missing.
- **NOT_REVIEWABLE:** the case cannot be assessed, for example because the source/candidate is too ambiguous to identify an offer. Give the reason; distinguish this from a plausible offer with insufficient historical evidence.

For resolved decisions provide the document title/URL or local reference, exact page/section, period of applicability to the origin, period of applicability to the target, and scope of the attributes/corridor covered. Effective dates can be explicit dates or supported intervals; an archive capture date alone does not establish when a product term applied. Retrospective documents are admissible if they explicitly establish earlier effective terms. A modern provider landing page or corporate annual report listing many markets generally cannot identify the exact historical bundle.

## Source guide and limits

Start with historical provider terms, dated product announcements, relevant regulator documents and official RPW documentation. Use lawful archives where accessible; log an inaccessible source rather than bypass controls. Do not contact authors/providers, purchase access or infer unavailable historical terms.

The World Bank's [methodology](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/methodology) explains the source variables and snapshot design. Its [June 2021 report, printed page 5](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/rpw_main_report_and_annex_q221.pdf) documents separation of combined instrument/access services. Its [December 2022 report, printed page 4](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/rpw_main_report_and_annex_q422_final.pdf) documents further separate combination recording. These are contextual sources, not case-level identity gold standards. Do not assume every apparent change at those boundaries is an artefact.

The automated preparation has not assigned identity decisions or supplied a target judgment. No case-specific historical adjudication has been completed by an independent human. If you cannot establish identity for many cases, retain UNRESOLVED rather than forcing a positive validation.
