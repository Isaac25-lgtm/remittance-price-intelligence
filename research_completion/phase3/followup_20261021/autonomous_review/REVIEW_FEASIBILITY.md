# Historical documentation feasibility

## Completed investigation and result

All **60** unique cases have case-specific AI reasoning, counterargument, alternative-candidate discussion and a final machine status. Every case remains unresolved. Grade A: **0/60 (0%)**; B: **2/60 (3.33%)**; C: **47/60 (78.33%)**; D: **11/60 (18.33%)**. B means partial near-period corridor support, never verified full product identity. D means no accessible pertinent product evidence in this bounded search, never proof that a service did not exist. No case is dropped or relabelled NOT_REVIEWABLE merely because its target is absent.

The investigation logged **72 discovery queries in 18 batches**, covering the **39 provider labels** in the blinded file, with targeted follow-up searches for historical and regulator leads. Queries used public corporate names/years/routes; no case IDs, full rows, outcomes or CSV uploads were sent to a search service. Provider sources were shared across relevant cases rather than counted as independent searches. The private case-source ledger has **185 rows**. It retains dates, full URLs, publishers, scope, paraphrases, locators, hashes, access outcomes and limitations for successful and failed leads.

For **56 discovered source URLs**, ordinary serial acquisition retained **31 original-byte reading copies**, including one reused checksum-verified report. The other **25** attempts failed: 22 ordinary access/network failures, one stalled request stopped without retry, one incomplete-response child-process failure and one total-deadline timeout. The source reader provided usable text for **9** of those failed originals. Those are text captures from the same source URLs, not nine additional independent documents or hashes of original PDFs. Overall, 40 source locations have original bytes or usable reader text; this includes duplicate report editions, generic pages and nonpertinent redirects. HTTP 200 alone is never documentary support.

One ordinary Wayback availability API probe returned HTTP 429. Archive requests stopped. No CAPTCHA, rate limit, paywall or credential protection was bypassed. This shared archive barrier applies to the investigation; it is not 39 separate archive searches and does not prove a historical page is absent. Source reader/tool-route failures and additional inaccessible probes are recorded privately. No author, provider or journal was contacted.

## Why no exact bundle was established

The strongest two cases supplied a year-specific service/corridor description or near-period effective product terms. Neither documented the full observed funding/access/payout/speed/network bundle continuously across both relevant quarters. One contained a material funding-vocabulary discrepancy. It remains partial support rather than a verified different product. Generic current terms, global annual reports and retrospective corporate history supplied context for most other cases.

The adversarial assessment distinguishes a payment rail from a branded remittance product, a provider affiliate from an exact legal entity, a document date from a continuous effective interval, a current website from an historical capture, and a stated country footprint from an ordered-corridor offer. An app article's actual displayed date differed from an anniversary year in its boilerplate. A requested historical PDF redirected to a current corporate page. Some service pages now contain only navigation. These failures were recorded rather than silently accepted as historical sources.

Rebranding, corporate administration or service closure can explain possible recording changes. A later UK regulatory restriction does not prove a global brand exited every country. A closure notice cannot supply an otherwise missing next-quarter price. Equal source labels or a unique candidate cannot prove stable economic identity. Several possible successors cannot be resolved by a global provider report. No alternative was rejected as a verified nonmatch without the required documentary scope.

## Blindness, freeze and audit

The randomized existing case order and human fields were preserved. The assessor saw origin/target labels, multiplicity and supplied alternative descriptions, but did not open the stratum key or outcome-bearing cache before sealing decisions. The first pass was performed by the AI assistant using fixed prompt/schema version `R01_AUTONOMOUS_DOCUMENTARY_V1`. The second pass was a deterministic adversarial scope/temporal/uniqueness audit. It is neither a second independent researcher nor an inter-rater validation. Its agreement with conservative proposals has no external-validity interpretation.

Search indexes and current marketing pages can surface fee text. One indexed RPW origin-fee hit was excluded; later queries excluded that domain. No RPW row page was opened for the evidence pass, no successor affordability outcome was used, and fees in product schedules were not converted to study outcomes. This was outcome-cache/stratum blinding, not a claim that the assistant never encountered any incidental price text.
