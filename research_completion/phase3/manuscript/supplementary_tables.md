# Supplementary numerical tables

All editable CSVs are under ../tables/ and ../identity/. LINKAGE_SCENARIO_RESULTS.csv contains all eight fixed basket/threshold/identity combinations plus E3 rows. wave_measurement.csv retains every eligible-origin wave. measurement_sensitivities.csv contains every broad stratum and fixed survey/dominance/weighting sensitivity, including adverse differences. paired_wave_identity.csv provides the same-origin paired contrast. Unknown states, canonicalisation and unit-support tables retain denominators. Files prefixed phase 2_ are exact safe aggregate copies, not rerun experiments.

Model metrics, calibration, annual comparisons, decision bounds, MAR/IPW, tipping points and all dependence sensitivities remain in the inherited supporting Phase 2 dossier. PRIMARY_CLAIM_ESTIMATES.csv and PAIRED_MEASUREMENT_EFFECTS.csv label new HAC sensitivities explicitly. No provider labels, source rows or individual predictions appear in these tables.
